How this practice tool works

  • Seven prompts per round, drawn from a bank of 20 categories written for this site.
  • 25 seconds per prompt. Type an answer and submit. Our validator checks a curated answer list with aliases and tolerates a one-letter typo on longer words.
  • Six tiers, same point values as the official game (10/15/30/60/85/100). They are assigned by our own editorial judgement of how obvious each answer is — they are not Krillion’s real tiers and cannot be compared with official scores.
  • Depth is points × 10. Seven top-tier answers make 7,000 m.
  • Nothing leaves your browser. There are no accounts, no cookies and no network calls; your best score is stored in localStorage only.

Why the answer bank is small

The official game validates tens of thousands of possible answers per prompt. Our bank recognises roughly 20–22 answers per prompt, so a perfectly valid answer can be missing. When that happens, the tool says so and lets you try another answer before the timer expires. Treat the bank as a drill, not as a dictionary.

What this tool will not do

  • It does not show today’s official prompts, answers or results.
  • It does not copy, embed or mirror krillion.io.
  • It does not claim to predict your official score. Use it to practise recall, category reading and the 25-second rhythm.
