Krillion Game: How to Play the Daily Dive

Krillion is a free daily trivia game played in the browser. Every day gives everyone the same seven open-answer prompts, about 25 seconds per prompt, and one rule that flips normal trivia on its head: the less obvious your valid answer, the deeper your submarine sinks.

How a dive works

  1. Open the Daily Dive. Seven prompts appear in the same order for every player worldwide.
  2. Type one answer per prompt. There are no multiple-choice buttons — you produce the answer yourself.
  3. Beat the timer. You get roughly 25 seconds per prompt. A blank or unrecognised entry scores nothing.
  4. Read your tier and sink. A valid answer lands in one of six rarity tiers; rarer tiers add more points and more depth.
  5. Finish all seven and compare. Your total depth is shareable. The Daily Dive is one scored attempt per day.

Scoring tiers

The exact tier depends on the prompt: the same word can be common for one question and rare for another. These are the six values the game uses:

Seven Plankton answers make 70 points (700 m). Seven Rare answers make 420 points (4,200 m). Seven perfect One in a Krillion answers make 700 points (7,000 m). That is why consistency beats gambling on one lucky answer.

What makes Krillion different from other trivia games?

Open answers, not multiple choice

You have to retrieve an answer from memory and spell it well enough for the validator.

Rarity is the score

Being correct is only the entry ticket. The points come from naming something other players did not.

A skill you can drill

Three or four minutes a day is enough to train recall, category reading and time management.

What Krillion is not

  • Not a quiz with one right answer. Most prompts accept a large set of valid answers; the game ranks them by rarity.
  • Not a daily-answer archive. This site does not publish today’s prompts or answers, and we do not scrape the official game.
  • Not the official site. Several unofficial domains use the Krillion name. The game’s own FAQ points players to krillion.io; treat everything else as a fan project.

Modes beyond the Daily Dive

The official site also links an Unlimited mode, Themed packs, an Archive of past dives and a beta party mode called Puffer Party. Modes and prices can change; check the official menu before you plan around them.

Frequently asked questions

What is Krillion?

Krillion is a free browser game where everyone gets the same seven open-answer trivia prompts each day. Valid answers score by rarity, and every point moves your dive 10 metres deeper toward a 7,000-metre trench floor.

Is Krillion free?

Yes. The Daily Dive is free to play in the browser. The official site also sells optional unlocks and shows ads, which can be removed with a purchase.

How long is a Krillion dive?

About three to four minutes: seven prompts with roughly 25 seconds each, plus the result screens.

When does Krillion reset?

A new dive appears each day. Player guides report the reset at 00:00 UTC; the countdown in the official client is authoritative.

Is this site the official Krillion game?

No. This is an independent guide and practice tool. The official game lives at krillion.io and is not affiliated with us.

Does the practice tool use real Krillion prompts?

No. Every prompt and answer in our practice dive was written for this site. Our tiers are editorial estimates, so scores are not comparable to the official game.

What is the maximum score in Krillion?

700 points, which equals 7,000 metres: seven answers in the top 100-point tier.
