About Krillion Game Guide

Krillion Game Guide is an independent fan project: a plain-language rules reference, a strategy guide and a free practice tool for a daily browser game we enjoy. It is not the official game and has no connection to its developer.

Who runs this site

This site is built and maintained by an independent developer as part of a small collection of free browser tools. We do not publish a personal name or a biography because there is no public author brand behind the site; the work is the site itself. If that changes, this page will say so.

Editorial policy

  • Facts carry sources. Rules, modes and scoring statements are checked against the official game at krillion.io and its FAQ, with the check date shown on the page.
  • Estimates are labelled. Our practice tiers are editorial estimates and are marked as such everywhere they appear.
  • No daily answers. We do not publish today’s prompts, answers or results, and we do not scrape the official game.
  • Corrections. If a rule or value changes, the official client wins; we update the page rather than defend an old claim.
  • No fake credentials. We do not claim to be the developer, an official partner or a professional trivia organisation.

What the practice tool is

The practice dive is our own implementation: an original question bank, a client-side validator, and a scoring model that mirrors the official tier values for training purposes. It runs entirely in your browser. It is not a clone of the official game, does not use its prompts or answer data, and does not reproduce its artwork or interface.

Intellectual property

“Krillion” and the official game belong to their developer. We use the name only to describe and discuss the game, with a clear non-affiliation notice on every page. We do not host, embed or redistribute the official game or its assets. If the owner has a concern about this fan site, we will respond and change or remove material as appropriate.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell anything, run ads or collect accounts.
  • We do not publish “daily answer” lists or spoilers.
  • We do not mirror other fan sites’ content.

Privacy

See the privacy page: no analytics, no cookies, no account, and only local browser storage for your practice best score.
